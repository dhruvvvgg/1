About SRMIST

A multidisciplinary university that began as one engineering college at Kattankulathur, near Chennai, and now has campuses across five Indian states.

Who we are.

SRM Institute of Science and Technology is one of India's leading universities. It teaches undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes in six faculties, on campuses in Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh and Sikkim.

Colleges and programmes

Vision and mission.

What the university sets out to be, and how it plans to get there.

Vision

To emerge as a world-class university in creating and disseminating knowledge, and in giving students a unique learning experience in science, technology, medicine, management and other areas of scholarship, to best serve the world and the betterment of mankind.

Mission

Move up through international alliances and collaborative initiatives to achieve global excellence.

Accomplish a process to advance knowledge in a rigorous academic and research environment.

Attract and build people in a rewarding and inspiring environment, by fostering freedom, empowerment, creativity and innovation.

Core values.

Three commitments run through the university's work.

  • Excellence, integrity and academic freedom

  • Global vision and local commitment

  • Inclusiveness, diversity, compassion and mutual respect

How SRM grew.

The SRM Group began in 1968. The university's own story starts in 1985.

History on the official site
  1. 1968The SRM Group of institutions is founded.
  2. 1985SRM Engineering College opens at Kattankulathur, near Chennai.
  3. 2002SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai, receives deemed-to-be-university status under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. SRM Dental College, Ramapuram, is included.
  4. 2003SRM Engineering College, Kattankulathur, comes under the university.
  5. 2010The Inter Disciplinary School of Indian System of Medicine is set up.
  6. 2013The university launches its campus in Haryana.
  7. 2024NAAC accredits SRMIST with its highest grade, A++.
