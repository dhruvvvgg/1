    Overview

    SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) have taken various research initiatives over the years to mobilize the available knowledge resources for research and also, to come up strongly with innovative solutions.

    • Committed to fostering and furthering research excellence
    • Specially focused on emerging areas of great potential like Nanotechnology, Bioengineering, Energy, Environment, Materials and Embedded systems among others
    • Actively involved in research projects that have nation building and public cause at their heart like for instance rural healthcare, among others.

    Mission

    1. Provide excellent research culture and infrastructure

    2. Serve as a platform for strong interdisciplinary collaborations and knowledge sharing

    3. Publish research findings in high quality journals of international repute

    4. Create scientifically profound human resources for academic & industrial research

    5. Promote industrial collaborations involving active and mutually beneficial R & D projects

    Central research facilities

    • IIISM (opens the original SRMIST site)

      Interdisciplinary Institute of Indian System of Medicine

      To promote the research in the field of traditional system of Indian Medicine, including Siddha, Ayurveda and Unani, IIISM was established in 2008.

    • REACH (opens the original SRMIST site)

      Center for Research in Environment, Sustainability Advocacy and Climate CHange

      REACH was initiated at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur, Tamil Nadu to support and enhance a cohesive university-wide environment of research excellence.

    • NRC (opens the original SRMIST site)

      Nanotechnology Research Center

      The Nanotechnology Research Center (NRC) with its state-of-the-art facilities is committed to the responsible development of nanotechnology as an important part of its environmental, health and safety research strategy.

    • CACR (opens the original SRMIST site)

      Center for Advanced Concrete Research

      The Center for Advanced Concrete Research (CACR) was started in the year 2010, to carry out interdisciplinary research in the field of concrete.

    • SRM-DBT Platform (opens the original SRMIST site)

      Established in collaboration with the Department of Biotechnology, Govt. of India: SRM-DBT Partnership Platform for Contemporary Research Services and Skill Development in Advanced Life Science Technologies.

    • Medical Research Centre (opens the original SRMIST site)

      SRMIST’s MRC, established in 2008, promotes research in medical & allied sciences.

    • Center for Statistics (opens the original SRMIST site)

      SRMIST’s Centre for Statistics is the hub of Statistical Training, Research and Consultancy.

    • Earthquake Research Cell (opens the original SRMIST site)

      Earthquake Research Cell (ERC) established in 2012 to decipher causes for origin, occurrence and prediction of Earthquake by better understanding the geodynamical behavior of this natural disaster on the globe.

    • HPCC (opens the original SRMIST site)

      High Performance Computing Centre

      A supercomputer facility for research work, with computational capacity far above a general-purpose computer.

    • SCIF (opens the original SRMIST site)

      SCIF portal consolidates the state-of-the-art research facilities available under the Directorate of Research in SRMIST and allows users to directly request & reserve time for use.
