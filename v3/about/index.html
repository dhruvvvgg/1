    2. About SRM

    SRM in focus

    Full time students
    60000+
    Faculty
    4460+

    SRM Institute of Science and Technology is one of the top ranking universities in India with over 60000+ full time students and more than 4460+ faculty across all the campuses – Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Baburayanpettai Campus.

    Vision

    To emerge as a World – Class University in creating and disseminating knowledge and providing students a unique learning experience in Science, Technology, Medicine, Management and other areas of Scholarship that will best serve the world and for the betterment of humanity.

    Mission

    1. MOVE UP through international alliances and collaborative initiatives to achieve global excellence.

    2. ACCOMPLISH A PROCESS to advance knowledge in a rigorous academic and research environment.

    3. ATTRACT AND BUILD PEOPLE in a rewarding and an inspiring environment by fostering freedom, empowerment, creativity and innovation

    Core values

    1. Excellence, Integrity and Academic Freedom

    2. Global Vision and Local Commitment

    3. Inclusiveness, Diversity, Compassion and Mutual Respect

    History

    1. 1985

      Launch of SRM Engineering College in Kattankulathur, near Chennai.

    2. 2002

      Sec. 3 of the UGC Act 1956 Deemed Status to SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai (incl SRM Dental College, Ramapuram)

    3. 2003

      SRM Engineering College, Kattankulathur, Chennai was brought under the ambit of SRMIST, Chennai, a deemed to be University.

    4. 2010

      Inter Disciplinary School of Indian System of Medicine.

    5. 2013

      Launch of SRM University, Haryana

    Recognition

    1. SRMIST Stands 4th in Country Wise and 147 in World Wide Ranking by UI Metric.

    2. SRMIST has been accredited by NAAC with the Highest ‘A++’ Grade in the year 2024.

    3. SRMIST holds the consecutive No. 1 position among the top engineering institutions, top 125 private engineering institutes, and the top 70 private institutes in placement respectively.

    4. found a podium finish in Atal Rankings of Institutions on Innovation Achievements (ARIIA) 2020.
