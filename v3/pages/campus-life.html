New to campus?

Ask your student team or department about recognised clubs, upcoming introductions and ways to take part. You do not need to arrive with everything figured out.

Make time to move.

Sport, athletics and fitness sit alongside academic life on campus.

Play, train or try something new

Ask the sports team about facilities, practice times, teams and the activities open to you. Access and schedules should be checked locally.

Be part of a team

Whether you want to develop a skill or meet people through an activity, start by finding out how to participate in campus sport.

Settle into everyday life.

Hostels, food, transport and campus facilities shape the practical side of university.

Hostels & accommodation

SRM has hostel facilities. Ask about current availability, allocation, charges and residence rules before making plans to move.

Dining & daily routines

Discuss dining arrangements, timings and any specific requirements with the relevant hostel or campus team.

Getting around

Get familiar with campus routes, transport arrangements and safety guidance after you arrive. Your student or residence team can help with local information.

You can ask for help.

Student support includes counselling, campus safety, grievance support and anti-ragging arrangements.

Start a conversation

Speak with your department, student support or residence team when you need guidance. Ask the university to connect you with student counselling when appropriate.
