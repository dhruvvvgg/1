    International Relations

    Office of International Relations is a dedicated directorate within SRMIST tasked with the mandate of ensuring best of the global practices, opportunities and exchange for the benefit of our students as well as faculty / staff.

    1. Home
    2. International Relations

    Vision

    To make and sustain the SRM Institution to be one of the top leading educational and research institutions in the world and actively contribute to achieving a position within the top 500 QS (Quacquarelli Symonds) Ranking with stronger collaboration with leading International Universities and Research Laboratories for Academics and Research

    Mission

    1. Sign MoUs with top 500 Universities and Research Laboratories to support students and faculty in Academics and Research.

    2. Increase the number of Research and other Academic engagements for students and faculty

    3. Bring Inclusiveness and Diversity by admitting more international students

    International Advisory Board

    Board set up
    2003
    Founding members
    31
    Members now
    50+

    Back in 2003, SRM set up an International Advisory Board (IAB) consisting of 31 Distinguished Deans, Directors and professors from top universities across the world. SRM’s IAB is the first across the Indian University system. Now over 50+ Members are on IAB and they are from the best of the universities across the world including Harvard Business School, Kennedy Public School (Harvard University), MIT, Stanford, LSE, UC Berkeley, Cambridge, NUS among others.

    Global partnership

    Universities and research centres
    100+

    Through strategic alliances with over 100 universities and research centers across continents, SRM enables academic mobility, collaborative research, dual degree programs, and international internships.
