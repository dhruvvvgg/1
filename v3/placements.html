The Career Centre.

It combines the Placement Office and the Career Development Centre.

Placement Office

Runs placement and training, with workshops, hackathons, quizzes, conferences, expert talks, internships and industrial visits, so students are market ready by graduation.

Career Development Centre

Helps students identify their interests and capabilities in relation to career goals and academic progression, with coaching in personality development and leadership.
