Build towards your next step.

Career preparation takes shape throughout your time at university, from your first projects to your first interviews.

  1. 01

    Know your direction

    Explore roles connected to your subject, speak to your academic team and identify the skills you want to develop.

  2. 02

    Show your work

    Build a clear CV or portfolio around projects, practical experience and what you contributed. Practise explaining your work.

  3. 03

    Prepare to meet employers

    Work on interviews, communication and role-specific preparation. Ask your placement team about current processes and requirements.

Experience makes learning tangible.

Know what to expect.

Recruitment is a process of preparation, eligibility and selection.

Does a recruiter logo guarantee a current opening?

No. These logos are examples from SRM’s existing recruiter list. Current openings, eligibility and hiring processes must be confirmed with the placement team.

Where do I find my programme’s recruitment calendar?

Speak to your department or placement coordinator. Schedules and registration requirements can differ by programme and employer.

Are internships and full-time placements the same process?

They can have different requirements and timelines. Ask which opportunities are available for your qualification and year of study.
