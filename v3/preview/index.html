How SRM grew.

From one engineering college at Kattankulathur to a university with campuses in six cities.

Our history
  1. 1985SRM Engineering College opens at Kattankulathur, near Chennai.
  2. 2002SRM Institute of Science and Technology receives deemed-to-be-university status under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956.
  3. 2003SRM Engineering College, Kattankulathur, comes under the university.
  4. 2010The Inter Disciplinary School of Indian System of Medicine is set up.
  5. 2024NAAC accredits SRMIST with its highest grade, A++.
