A question to make your own.

Doctoral study begins with a research interest and a conversation about its academic fit.

  1. 01

    Define your interest

    Describe the question, problem or area you want to explore and why it matters to your field.

  2. 02

    Explore academic fit

    Identify the relevant discipline and discuss potential supervision, methods and facilities.

  3. 03

    Confirm the route

    Ask about eligibility, selection and the current doctoral admission process before preparing an application.
