    Founder's words

    SRMIST’s engineering programs endeavor to be at the forefront of innovation. They also foster multi-disciplinary collaborations aimed at solving the most pressing global problems.

    Our goal is to deliver world class, solutions driven programs that inspire curiosity and generate new knowledge and discoveries. Our collaboration with over 50 of the world’s best universities and 215 corporates, strengthens our academic and research programs.

    Dean's message

    Welcome to the College of Engineering & Technology at SRMIST! At SRMIST, we are committed to academic excellence, research innovation, and holistic student development.

    Beyond academics, we nurture a vibrant campus culture that fosters creativity, critical thinking, and ethical leadership.

    Dr. Leenus Jesu Martin M

    Dean – FET, Kattankulathur

    Pathways

    A student shall have an option of choosing any one of the following pathways: (i) Major (ii) Major with Specialization (iii) Major with Minor or Major with specialization and a Minor. The core philosophy practiced is Inter-Disciplinary Experiential Active Learning (IDEAL).
