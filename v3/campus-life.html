Campus life

Sports, hostels, festivals and student support on a green campus of more than 250 acres.

Sports and fitness.

There are activities for every interest, from sport to fine arts and entrepreneurship. SRMIST sports students have secured prestigious public-sector positions.

Sports at SRM

Hostels and dining.

SRMIST provides housing in several room types, with an emphasis on safety, and a range of mess facilities and cafeterias.

Clubs and festivals.

Cultural events, student clubs and technical teams keep the campus busy long after classes end. Aaruush is SRM's techno-management fest, and Milan is its cultural festival.

Clubs and societies

Student support and safety.

Walking and cycling are the popular ways to get around, with campus security and 24-hour emergency services.

Counselling and support

The university offers student counselling and a Socio-Economically Disadvantaged Groups (SEDG) Cell.

Counselling services

Anti-ragging

SRM strongly condemns ragging in any form. Students welcome freshers with flowers and advice.
